# Ravanan Thirumbittaan - a write-up of the melody and the song

*Composed by Claude Work for the Agentic Band, 7 Nov 2026. Krish (director) approved the words: "Words are fine." Our copyrighted
material. Every number below comes from the code that makes the music (`raagacomposer/studio/answer.py`, `answer2.py`, `song.py`).*

## The story it tells
A young artist walks on and the crowd has already given up on him; they boo. He asks for ten seconds. In those ten seconds he
turns the room, and by the end the whole town is on its feet. It is a Ravana Returns scene: the one everyone wrote off comes back.

## Raaga: Shanmukhapriya
| | |
|---|---|
| Melakarta | 56th |
| Arohanam | S R2 G2 M2 P D1 N2 S |
| Avarohanam | S N2 D1 P M2 G2 R2 S |
| Notes in this song | on D (S = D) for most of it, then on E after the lift |

**Why this raaga.**
1. **It was already in your clip.** The funky TikTok clip you gave me as inspiration has a hook whose colour, heard over its own
   tonic, is S R2 G2 M2 P D1 N2. That is Shanmukhapriya. I took the feel and the raaga from the clip, not its melody: every note of
   the tune here is new.
2. **It suits the story.** Shanmukhapriya has the *prati madhyamam* (M2), the sharp Ma, sitting a tritone away from Sa. Played
   against Sa it is uneasy, which is the boos. Resolved up to Pa it is bold, which is the turn. With komal D1 and N2 above Pa, the
   upper half sounds heroic and a little dark: right for Ravana.
3. **It is not the earlier draft's raaga.** The first song-team sketch on 7 Nov was in Natabhairavi. This song is Shanmukhapriya.

## Tala and pulse
* **Tempo**: 140 beats a minute, four beats a bar, four sixteenths a beat (chatusra nadai).
* **Adi tala**: two bars are one cycle of Adi tala's eight aksharas. Phrases start on a bar line - except the veena's deliberately
  late entry in groove A - and the big hits land on sam.
* **Khanda nadai**: the break groups the sixteenths in fives (5 + 5 + 6 across the bar) against the clap's steady four.
* **Tihais**: two of them.
  * The answer tihai is P D1 N2 three times: 3 + (3 x 3) + (2 x 2 gaps) = 16 steps, landing on sam with the whole band.
  * The ending tihai is S+ N2 D1 P three times: (3 x 4) + (2 x 2 gaps) = 16 steps, landing on the final Sa.
  You said the ending was powerful; it stays as it is in every version.

## The tune (the hook)
Two bars of call (veena), two of answer (saxophone in v4/v5, flute in v6). It is written on a sixteen-step bar; numbers are the
step each note starts on, and accents fall 3 + 3 + 2.

| Bar | Notes (step:swara) |
|---|---|
| Call 1 | 0:S+ 3:N2 4:D1 6:P 9:M2 10:P 13:D1 |
| Call 2 | 0:N2 1:D1 2:P 4:M2 5:G2 6:M2 10:P 12:D1 13:N2 14:S+ |
| Answer 1 | 0:R2+ 3:S+ 6:N2 8:D1 9:P 10:D1 12:P |
| Answer 2 | 0:M2 1:P 2:M2 3:G2 4:R2 6:G2 8:S (held) |
| Answer, flying up | 0:M2 1:P 2:M2 3:G2 4:R2 6:G2 8:P 10:N2 12:S+ |

The call climbs down from upper Sa to Pa and back up. The answer starts on upper Ri, falls to Sa, and comes home. The second time
round, the answer refuses to come home and flies up to upper Sa instead.

## Sangatis: how the hook changes each time
1. **First statement, bare**: the veena alone at 1.7 s, only claps under it.
2. **Answer over stop-time**: the answer comes over string stabs that climb along the raaga.
3. **Late entry** (groove A, second half): the veena's call comes a beat late over 3-against-4 hats; the answer stays on the beat.
4. **Half speed** (charanam): the call at half speed over a half-time beat, with the violin an octave above.
5. **The violin an octave over the answer** in groove B. (In v4, the violin doubled the call a third higher along the raaga; v5
   and v6 use the octave instead.)
6. **The answer flies up**: it ends on upper Sa instead of coming home to Sa.
7. **Octave up**: in groove B's second half the veena plays the call an octave higher.
8. **The lift**: everything moves up a whole step (D to E) for groove B, the same raaga on a new Sa.

## Structure (Ravana's Answer II, 55.6 s)
| Time | Section | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | Boos (1 bar) | A low choir moaning down the raaga; a heartbeat kick; Sa against prati Ma |
| 1.7 | His ten seconds (6 bars) | The pallavi on veena; the answer over stop-time; the tihai; sam; the pick-up |
| 12.0 | Groove A (8 bars) | The hook with the band; then the late entry over 3:4 hats |
| 25.7 | Half-time (4 bars) | The floor drops; the charanam; the hook at half speed |
| 32.6 | Khanda break (2 bars) | Fives; the hand drum (mridangam in v5, tabla in v6a, bongos in v6b), then tabla; the veena in fives |
| 36.0 | Groove B (8 bars) | Up a whole step; double-time hats; call and answer a bar each; whistles |
| 49.7 | Tihai (2 bars) | Three times with everyone; the last Sa; whistles over the ring-out |

### Vallinam, mellinam, idaiyinam
Krish's rule:
* **Vallinam** (க ச ட த ப ற) is hard: fierce, a march.
* **Mellinam** (ங ஞ ண ந ம ன) is soft: melodious.
* **Idaiyinam** (ய ர ல வ ழ ள) sits between.

| Line | Vallinam | Mellinam | Idaiyinam |
|---|---|---|---|
| கேளு கேளு கேளுடா | 4 | 0 | 3 |
| காத்திருந்த நாட்கள் முடிந்தது | 9 | 4 | 2 |
| ராவணன் திரும்பிட்டான் | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| கைதட்டும் நாள் வருது | 5 | 2 | 3 |
| ஊரெல்லாம் எழுந்து ஆடுது | 3 | 2 | 4 |
| கூச்சல் போட்ட கூட்டமே | 9 | 1 | 1 |
| பத்து நொடி கொடு என்னை பாரு | 7 | 3 | 1 |
| ராவணன்டா | 1 | 2 | 2 |

The song leans hard: 42 vallinam letters against 18 mellinam. That fits this scene: a fierce, march-like turn of a hostile crowd,
in a heroic raaga. Two places deliberately soften:
* the name ராவணன், which is evenly balanced (4 / 4 / 3);
* the town rising and dancing (ஊரெல்லாம் எழுந்து ஆடுது), the most idaiyinam line.

The jeering crowd (கூச்சல் போட்ட கூட்டமே) is the hardest line (9 vallinam), on purpose. A softer, melodious version - say, a love
song in Sindhu Bhairavi - would rewrite toward mellinam.
